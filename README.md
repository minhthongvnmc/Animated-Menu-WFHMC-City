# 📌 General Information
Project Name: Animated Menu: WFHMC City
Initial Release Date: August 8, 2026
Author / Studio: MinhthongVNMC Studio

This image transforms Minecraft's default startup screen into a stunning and colorful cityscape. It's not just a typical Minecraft building map, but a perfect blend of innovative construction techniques and advanced graphics, creating a peaceful and livable WFHMC City (a familiar Minecraft city).

Key Features:

Vibrant Natural Scenery:

Lush green trees surround rustic and cozy wooden houses.

Blossoms of flowers, especially vibrant red roses, add striking color accents.

A clear, cloudless sky perfectly reflects on the calm waters of a lake or canal, creating beautiful symmetry and depth in the scene.

Realistic Lighting and Shadow Effects (Shader):

The use of shader packs makes sunlight appear more realistic than ever.  Sunlight filters through the leaves, creating distinct patches of light and shadow.

The water not only reflects the sky but also refracts light, making the surface shimmer and appear more vibrant.

A familiar Minecraft city:

The village with its wooden houses, fences, and lampposts creates a sense of familiarity, like a peaceful residential area recreated in the blocky world.

The red flag with a yellow star is a special highlight, representing national pride or a familiar Vietnamese community within the game.

User interface:

All menu options (Play, Settings, Skins, Friends...) are retained and easy to read, ensuring players can navigate as easily as the default screen.

The game version (Minecraft v26.40) and Mojang Studios' license are clearly displayed in the bottom right corner.
